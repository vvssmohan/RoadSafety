Basic Road Safety Rules

1. Follow Traffic Signals

Always obey traffic lights. Red means stop, yellow means prepare to stop, and green means go.

2. Wear Seatbelts

Always wear your seatbelt while driving or riding in a vehicle. It can save your life.

3. Follow Speed Limits

Drive within the speed limit. Speed limits are set for your safety and others.

4. Don't Use Mobile Phones

Never use your mobile phone while driving. It can cause serious accidents.

5. Give Way to Pedestrians

Always stop at zebra crossings and let pedestrians cross safely.

6. Wear Helmets

If you ride a bike or motorcycle, always wear a helmet for protection.
