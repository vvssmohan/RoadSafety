📚 Learn Road Safety

Master these essential road safety rules to keep yourself and others safe

🚦 Traffic Signals

RED Light

STOP - Do not proceed. Wait behind the stop line.

YELLOW Light

PREPARE TO STOP - Slow down, the light will turn red soon.

GREEN Light

GO - Proceed with caution if the intersection is clear.

🦺 Safety Equipment

  • Seatbelts: Wear seatbelts at all times. They reduce the risk of fatal injury by 50%.
  • Helmets: Mandatory for two-wheeler riders. Reduces head injury risk by 70%.
  • Child Seats: Children under 12 should use appropriate car seats or restraints.
  • Reflective Clothing: Wear bright or reflective clothing at night for visibility.

🚶 Pedestrian Safety

  • Always use zebra crossings or foot overbridges to cross roads
  • Look left, right, and left again before crossing
  • Make eye contact with drivers before crossing
  • Never run across the road
  • Avoid using mobile phones while walking on roads

🚗 Driving Best Practices

  • Maintain Distance: Keep safe distance from the vehicle ahead (3-second rule)
  • Use Indicators: Always signal before turning or changing lanes
  • No Drunk Driving: Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Avoid Distractions: Don't use mobile phones while driving
  • Night Driving: Use headlights and drive slowly at night
  • Bad Weather: Reduce speed in rain, fog, or adverse conditions
