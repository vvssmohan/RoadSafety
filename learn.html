Road Safety Basics

Remember these simple rules every time you travel.

  • Follow traffic lights: Red (Stop), Yellow (Prepare), Green (Go)
  • Wear seatbelts and helmets for protection
  • Keep to speed limits, especially in school zones
  • Give way to pedestrians at zebra crossings
  • Avoid mobile phones while driving or walking on roads
  • Use indicators before turning or changing lanes
  • Maintain safe distance: the 3-second rule

Signs & Signals

Stop Sign

Come to a complete stop, proceed when safe.

Speed Limit

Stay at or below the posted limit.

Zebra Crossing

Yield to pedestrians crossing the road.

Traffic Lights

Red=Stop, Yellow=Prepare, Green=Go cautiously.
