Basic Road Safety Rules

1. Follow Signals

Red = Stop, Yellow = Prepare, Green = Go.

2. Wear Seatbelts

Always buckle up, even for short trips.

3. Obey Speed Limits

Slow in residential and school areas.

4. No Distractions

Avoid mobile phones while driving or walking.

5. Give Way

Stop for pedestrians at crossings.

6. Use Helmets

Always wear helmets on two-wheelers.
